trigger: always_on
description: UI and writing rules for RaktSetu Live. Always apply.
---
- Mobile first. Test at 375px. Tap targets at least 44px high.
- The public side has no login, no popups, no cookie walls, no required typing.
- Plain, short sentences in the user's reading level. Name actions by what happens: "Call bank", "Publish stock". Errors say what went wrong and what to do next. No apologies, no jargon.
- Colour never carries meaning alone: stock levels also show text ("Low: 3 units").
- Keep visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields, and `role="alert"` for errors.
- Do not add motion for decoration. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep the visual identity: blood red `#b3202f`, cool neutral background, stock tiles as the main visual element. Do not add gradients, stock photos or illustration.
